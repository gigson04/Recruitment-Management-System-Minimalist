# Hiring Manager Manual — Recruitment Management System

**Creator:** Gigson Aquino

## Reviewing candidates
1. Open Applications and review the candidate records available to your role.
2. Confirm that the latest screening is Qualified and that a completed interview is marked Passed.
3. Select **Select candidate** only when both requirements are met. The application status becomes Selected.
4. Coordinate with HR Staff before finalizing an offer or communicating a decision externally.

## Recording a decision
1. Open Hiring decisions → Record decision.
2. Select an application already marked Selected.
3. Choose Hired, Declined, Rejected, or On Hold.
4. Enter salary offer and start date only when approved and necessary.
5. Confirm the resulting application status with HR Staff. For a Hired decision, a passed completed interview is required. Follow organizational approval and notification procedures.

Do not create or change user roles. Keep salary and candidate data confidential. If a record is incorrect, coordinate with the authorized HR Staff or Administrator.
