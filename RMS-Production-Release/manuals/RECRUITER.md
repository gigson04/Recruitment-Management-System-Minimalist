# Recruiter Manual — Recruitment Management System

**Creator:** Gigson Aquino

## Your workflow
1. **Create a vacancy:** open Job postings → New posting. Fill in title, department, employment type, location, closing date, vacancies, description, and qualifications. Save only approved vacancy details.
2. **Publish or close:** an Open posting is visible to active Applicant accounts while its deadline is current. Close a vacancy when it is filled or no longer accepting submissions.
3. **Review applications:** open Applications, confirm the position and applicant, then update the status to reflect the actual workflow stage.
4. **Screen candidates:** enter the five weighted screening criteria. RMS totals the score and determines Qualified at 70 or above; the cutoff can be changed in `js/config.js` to match organization policy. Add factual remarks.
5. **Coordinate interviews:** schedule interviews, then enter all five interview criteria after the interview. RMS totals the score and determines Passed at 70 or above; the cutoff can be changed in `js/config.js`. Use the correct applicant/application ID.
6. **Track outcomes:** use Reports for current counts. Coordinate final hiring decisions with authorized HR Staff or Hiring Managers.

## Data protection
Use applicant information only for recruitment. Keep remarks professional and job-related. Do not send candidate data to unauthorized recipients or export it to public devices.
