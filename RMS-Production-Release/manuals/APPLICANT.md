# Applicant Manual — Recruitment Management System

**Creator:** Gigson Aquino

## Create an account
1. Choose **Create applicant account** on the sign-in page.
2. Enter your name, email, and a strong password with at least 10 characters.
3. Submit the form. If email confirmation is enabled, open the message sent to your email and confirm your address.
4. Ask the recruitment administrator to link your Supabase Auth account to your existing applicant record (`applicants.auth_user_id`). This is required by the current database access rules; the app does not change your database structure.
5. Sign in using the confirmed email and password after the account is linked.

## Find and apply for a job
1. Open **Job postings**.
2. Review the job title, department, employment type, location, deadline, description, and qualifications.
3. Select **Apply** for the position that matches your interests.
4. Enter your cover letter and, if requested, a secure HTTPS resume link. The current version does not upload resume files directly.
5. Submit once. The system prevents a second application for the same position.

## Track an application
- Open **My applications** to see your submitted applications and their current status.
- Statuses may include Submitted, Under Review, Screening, Interview, Selected, Hired, Rejected, or Withdrawn.
- Contact the recruitment office using its official contact method if you believe a status is incorrect.

## Password and privacy
- Choose **Forgot password?** to request a password reset link.
- Never share your password or email verification link.
- Provide accurate information. Use your application information only for legitimate recruitment purposes.
- Sign out when using a shared device.
