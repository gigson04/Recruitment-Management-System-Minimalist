# Interviewer Manual — Recruitment Management System

**Creator:** Gigson Aquino

## Before the interview
- Sign in using your assigned account.
- Open Interviews and review the interview assigned to you.
- Check the schedule and relevant job requirements through the approved recruitment contact.

## After the interview
- Enter the five interview criteria (Communication 20, Technical Knowledge 30, Problem Solving 20, Teamwork 15, Professionalism 15). The system totals the score out of 100 and calculates Passed at 70 or above, or Failed below 70. The cutoff can be changed in `js/config.js` to match organization policy. Also update the completion status and add concise factual remarks.
- Record only information relevant to the job criteria. Avoid sensitive personal details and unsupported judgments.
- If an interview is rescheduled or cancelled, update its status and notify the recruiting contact through the organization's approved channel.

## Access limits
Interviewers should only access interviews assigned to them. If the assignment is missing or incorrect, ask HR Staff or a Recruiter to correct it. Do not attempt to access another candidate's records by changing URLs or IDs.
