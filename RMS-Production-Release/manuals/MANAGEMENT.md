# Management Manual — Recruitment Management System

**Creator:** Gigson Aquino

## Dashboard and reports
- Sign in using your assigned Management account.
- Use Dashboard to review current system totals.
- Use Reports for live counts of job postings, applications, screenings, interviews, and hiring outcomes.
- Treat these as current totals, not a complete historical analytics package.

## Responsible use
Use summaries for authorized recruitment oversight. Avoid exporting identifiable applicant data unless there is an approved business need. Management accounts cannot change staff roles or administer authentication settings. Contact HR Staff or the Administrator for corrections and access changes.
